How Raket works

If you want work

  1. Create a free account. Choose “Find work” and add your phone number.
  2. Pick a job. Filter by kind of work, place and how long it lasts.
  3. Send a short note. The household reviews it and picks who to hire.
Find work

If you need help at home

  1. Create a free account. Choose “Hire help”.
  2. Post your job. Say what, where, when and how much you will pay.
  3. Choose who to hire. Applicants write to you, you decide.
